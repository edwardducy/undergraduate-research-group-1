# DSWD DROMIC Report #46 on the Effects of Enhanced Southwest Monsoon and Tropical Cyclone “Inday”

as of 10 September 2026, 6PM

## I. Situation Overview

Issued 07 July 2026 at 4AM, cloudy skies with scattered rains and thunderstorms due to the Southwest Monsoon (SWM) may have caused flash floods or landslides due to moderate to at times heavy rains in Mindanao. On the same day, the prevailing SWM also brought heavy rainfall and strong winds to Zamboanga del Sur, resulting in the cancellation of sea travel and leaving passengers stranded.

On 08 July 2026, Super Typhoon (STY) "Inday" (international name "Bavi") entered the Philippine Area of Responsibility (PAR). Although the typhoon remained offshore, it enhanced the prevailing SWM, bringing heavy rainfall and strong winds that affected several areas in the country.

In the Negros Island Region (NIR), the enhanced SWM caused the displacement of families and individuals due to heavy rainfall and strong winds. In Bukidnon, continuous heavy rains triggered flooding and landslide incidents in Malaybalay City and Valencia City, resulting in the displacement of families, damage to infrastructure, and one reported fatality. Meanwhile, in the Municipality of Quezon, flooding and landslides affected five (5) barangays, resulting in one fatality after an individual was buried by a landslide. The incident also damaged houses, agricultural areas, roads, bridges, and other infrastructure, with several road sections remaining impassable. Concerned authorities immediately conducted evacuation, response, clearing, assessment, and damage validation operations.

At around 4:00 PM, flooding occurred in Brgy. Dapok, Kiblawan, Davao del Sur due to continuous moderate to occasionally heavy rainfall, causing the collapse and washout of approximately 30 meters of concrete road. The damaged road remains unsafe for vehicular traffic, disrupting access to essential services.

At around 9:30 PM, sustained rainfall and strong winds brought by the SWM triggered flooding in Pigcawayan, Cotabato Province and Maitum, Sarangani. The same weather system caused flooding and landslides in high-risk areas of Kalamansig, Lebak, Lutayan, Esperanza, and Palimbang, Sultan Kudarat, resulting in the displacement of several families. Meanwhile, in Maasim, Sarangani, several families preemptively evacuated due to overflowing waterways caused by continuous heavy rains.

On 09 July 2026 at around 9:00 AM, moderate to heavy rains caused flooding in several low-lying barangays in the Province of Antique. In addition, flooding was reported in several Municipalities of Maguindanao del Norte and Maguindanao del Sur due to the effects of the Enhanced SWM. A landslide also occurred in Brgy. Poblacion, Malapatan, Sarangani, resulting in 10 casualties.

On 10 July 2026, several tourists were reported stranded in the Municipalities of Basco and Itbayat, Batanes, due to the cancellation of scheduled flights and the implementation of a no-sailing policy brought about by the adverse weather conditions associated with Tropical Cyclone (TC) Inday. Likewise, on 11 July 2026, additional passengers were reported stranded due to the continued suspension of air and sea transportation services in the Municipalities of Basco and Itbayat, Batanes.

In response, the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), through Field Offices (FOs), has already heightened its alert status, conducting 24/7 monitoring of weather advisories, hazard alerts, and field updates. This includes ready deployment of the Quick Response Teams (QRTs), and specialized vehicles for disaster response; and provision of food and non-food items (FNIs) for the affected population.

Further, the DSWD FOs II, CALABARZON, MIMAROPA, V, VI, NIR, IX, X, XI, and XII have already provided relief assistance to address the immediate needs of the affected families and individuals.

Source: DOST-PAGASA and DSWD FOs
