# PRESS RELEASE

# Over 68 Million Filipinos Aged 10 to 64 Years Used the Internet for Social Media in 2024

Date of Release: 06 October 2025

Reference No. 2025-318

The Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) releases its fourth report on the results of the 2024 Functional Literacy, Education, and Mass Media Survey (FLEMMS). This report presents the mass media exposure of the population aged 10 to 64 years in the country.

Mass Media refers to the technology that serves as the primary means of communication used to reach the vast majority of the general public. The most common platforms for mass media are newspapers, magazines, radio, television, and the internet. The general public typically relies on mass media to obtain information regarding political and social issues, entertainment, and news.

Exposure to mass media indicates that the respondent accessed specific forms of mass media whether every day, at least once a week, or seldom in the last 12 months preceding the survey.

In the 2024 FLEMMS, additional items such as online/digital newspaper, video streaming, music streaming, and internet for e-commerce, government services, financial transactions, and online banking were included as platforms for mass media exposure.

## 1. Online/digital newspapers recorded the highest exposure rate among reading platforms

Based on the 2024 FLEMMS results, online/digital newspapers recorded the highest exposure rate among the reading platforms for the population aged 10 to 64 years, at 52.1 percent. This was followed by printed magazines (50.2%), printed posters (49.2%), and printed newspapers (30.0%).

Compared to the previous FLEMMS round, the overall exposure to mass media through reading platforms declined in 2024, with percentage point decreases ranging from 19.9 (for printed posters) to 33.3 (for printed newspapers). (Figure 1 and Table 1)

At the regional level, Central Visayas posted the highest exposure rate for online/digital newspapers at 64.6 percent, while the lowest exposure to this medium was recorded in Zamboanga Peninsula at 39.2 percent. (Table 2a)

## 2. Television was the most widely accessed audio/visual platform in 2024

In the 2024 FLEMMS, categories for video and music streaming were added and measured along with traditional audio/visual platforms such as television, radio, and movies/plays/similar shows.

Among audio/visual platforms, television had the highest exposure rate at 82.3 percent among individuals aged 10 to 64 years, followed by video streaming at 66.5 percent, and music streaming at 63.5 percent. Movies, plays, and similar shows had a lower exposure rate of 58.2 percent, while radio was recorded the lowest at 52.9 percent. (Tables 1 and 2b)

Similar to the results of the reading platforms, the overall exposure rate to traditional audio/visual platforms decreased in 2024, with percentage point decreases ranging from 7.7 (for movies, plays, and similar shows) to 22.3 (for radio). (Figure 2 and Table 1)

Among the regions, National Capital Region (NCR) recorded the highest exposure rate to television at 88.0 percent, while MIMAROPA Region recorded the lowest exposure rate in the same platform at 75.3 percent. (Table 2b)

## 3. Four out of five Filipinos aged 10 to 64 years accessed the internet for social media

The 2024 FLEMMS results showed that four out of five Filipinos (80.5%) aged 10 to 64 years or approximately 68.42 million individuals have used the internet for social media. (Figure 3 and Tables 1 and 2c)

The second most common internet usage was for work or research purposes at 52.7 percent of the same age group population. Internet use for e-commerce platform followed at 45.3 percent, while 37.0 percent accessed the internet for government services, financial transactions or online banking. (Figure 3 and Tables 1 and 2c)

Among the regions, NCR had the highest rate of internet usage for accessing social media at 88.9 percent, while Zamboanga Peninsula recorded the lowest rate at 67.8 percent. (Table 2c)
