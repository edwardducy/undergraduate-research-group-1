# Writing Rules

- **Style guide**: Follow the APA Publication Manual (7th edition), except where the rules below or the department's format differ.
- **Perspective**: Do not use first-person pronouns (I, we, our, us) or second-person pronouns (you, your).
- **Punctuation**: Do not use semicolons (;), colons (:), or em dashes (—) in the body text.

# Course Materials

- **Course lessons and assignment instructions** (`canvas_docs/`): Meet the general shape and intent of each required section, such as its purpose and what it must cover. How a section achieves this is up to the authors. Do not treat the specific techniques, structures, or lengths in these materials, such as the Macro-Meso-Micro funnel or page counts, as requirements, because the professor stated that the materials are wrong on these points.
