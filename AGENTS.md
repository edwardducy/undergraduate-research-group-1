# AGENTS.md

Writing rules for this thesis. Follow every rule when writing or editing text.
The authors and the audience are non-native English speakers.

- Don't use em dashes.
- Don't use semi-colons in sentences. Use them only between citations in one pair of brackets.
- Avoid using colons unless they introduce a list on a new line.
- Avoid writing longer than 30 words per sentence.
- Use literal verbs instead of idioms and phrasal verbs, such as replacing "carry out" with "conduct".
- Don't use first-person or second-person pronouns, such as "we", "our", and "you".
