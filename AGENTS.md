# Project Instructions

## Manuscript Writing Rules

These rules apply only to the manuscript files (`*.qmd`).

- **Style guide**: Follow the APA Publication Manual (7th edition), except where the rules below or the department's format differ.
- **Perspective**: Do not use first-person pronouns (I, we, our, us) or second-person pronouns (you, your).
- **Punctuation**: Do not use semicolons (;), colons (:), or em dashes (—) in the body text. The Definitions of Terms may use a colon after each term.
