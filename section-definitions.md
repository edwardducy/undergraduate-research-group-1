# Section Definitions

## Chapter 1

| Section | Definition |
|---|---|
| 1.1 Introduction to the Study | A short overview of the problem and what the study does about it |
| 1.2 Background of the Study | The context and prior work that lead to the problem, ending with the gap the study fills |
| 1.3 Statement of the Problem | The general problem and the specific research questions the study answers |
| 1.4 Objectives | What the study sets out to do, one objective for each research question |
| 1.5 Hypotheses | The expected answers to the research questions that are tested statistically |
| 1.6 Theoretical Framework | The theories or established ideas the study builds on and how they explain the expected results |
| 1.7 Conceptual Framework | A diagram of the study's variables or inputs, process, and outputs, and how they relate |
| 1.8 Scope and Delimitations | What the study covers and what it deliberately leaves out, with reasons |
| 1.8.1 Scope of the Study | What the study covers |
| 1.8.2 Delimitations of the Study | What the study leaves out and why |
| 1.9 Significance of the Study | Who benefits from the results and what each gains from them |
| 1.10 Definitions of Terms | The meaning of key terms as the study uses them |

## Chapter 2

| Section | Definition |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Natural Language Processing of Disaster Posts | How earlier studies and systems classified disaster posts and extracted their key details, and what is missing for Taglish posts |
| 2.2 Multi-Task Learning | The idea the study builds on, the problem it measures, and the methods it compares |
| 2.3 Algorithmic Literature Taxonomy Matrix | A table that compares the reviewed studies side by side |
| 2.4 Synthesis of Related Literature and Studies | The trends across the reviewed studies and the gaps the study fills |

## Chapter 3

| Section | Definition |
|---|---|
| 3.1 Research Methodology | The kind of study and how its steps answer the research questions |
| 3.2 Subjects of the Study | The data and models being studied, described so a reader knows what the results apply to |
| 3.3 Sampling Technique | How the data is picked from everything available and split into training, validation, and test sets |
| 3.4 Instruments to Be Used | The tools that produce every reported number, and what each one measures |
| 3.5 Procedure of Data Gathering | The steps in order, from collecting the data to recording the results |
| 3.6 Statistical Treatment | The tests that show whether differences between models are real or due to chance |
| 3.7 Data Analysis | The formula for every reported metric and how the results are read |
| 3.8 Software System Description | The software built in the study and what it runs on |
| 3.8.1 System Structure | The parts of the system and how data moves between them |
| 3.8.2 Use Cases | Who uses the system and what they do with it |
| 3.8.3 Materials and Equipment | The hardware and software versions needed to repeat the study |
| 3.9 Testing and Evaluation | How the software is checked to work and how it performs under load |
